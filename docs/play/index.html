Play

  1. Click Start sound on this page.
  2. Scan the code with a phone's camera (or open the link and type the room code). Pick Flute or Theremin.
  3. Flute: hold the phone sideways, tilt for the note, blow on the bottom edge. Theremin: phone flat on a table, move a book up and down above its bottom edge.
  4. Anyone can join: in the room or from home. Several phones can play at once; each gets a card below.

Showing it in a video call

  1. Join the call from this laptop. Never from a phone that's playing: the call app takes over its microphone.
  2. Share this tab with its sound. Google Meet: Present now → A tab → this tab, keep "Also share tab audio" on. Zoom: Share → tick "Share sound". Teams: Share → "Include sound".
  3. People in the call can scan the code off their screen and play too. They hear themselves through the call, about half a second late.
  4. Phones in the same room as the laptop: wear headphones on the laptop, so the flute's mic hears only breath.

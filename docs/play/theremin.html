Hand Theremin

This phone is the sensor; the stage on the laptop plays the notes. Sonar, no camera.
  • Lay the phone flat on the table, screen up. Phone volume all the way up, silent mode off.
  • Play over the phone's bottom edge (the speaker end). A book held flat works best; a hand reaches about 14 cm.
  • After Start, keep everything away from the phone for 1 second while it measures the empty table.
  • Lowest note at about 2 cm above the phone, highest at about 24 cm.

Most adults can't hear the 18–21 kHz chirp, but some people (especially younger ones) and pets can. If it bothers anyone, tap Stop. Don't use headphones.

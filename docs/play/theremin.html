Hand Theremin

Sonar, no camera: the phone chirps above hearing and times the echo off a book or your hand.
  • Lay the phone flat on the table, screen up. Volume up, silent mode off.
  • Play over the phone's bottom edge (the speaker end). A book held flat works best (up to about 35 cm); a hand reaches about 14 cm.
  • After Start, keep everything away from the phone for 1 second while it measures the empty table.

Most adults can't hear the 18–21 kHz chirp, but some people (especially younger ones) and pets can. If it bothers anyone, tap Stop. Don't use headphones.

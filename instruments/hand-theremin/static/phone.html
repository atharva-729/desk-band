Hand Theremin

Phase 0: sonar check. No music yet.

Lay the phone flat on the table, screen up. Turn the volume up and silent mode off.

Tap Start. The phone plays a very high, quiet chirp and listens for its echo off your hand, like a bat.

Play with your hand over the bottom edge of the phone (the speaker grille end): that's where the speaker and the mic are.

Most adults can't hear 18–21 kHz, but some people (especially younger ones) and pets can. If it bothers anyone, tap Stop. Don't use headphones.

Keep your hand away: measuring the empty table…

How to read this

  • The phone plays a chirp every 21 ms and times its echo. Longer wait = higher hand.
  • For the first second it measures the empty table, then shows only what changed. So with no hand, the map stays dark. It only measures again when you tap Re-measure on the phone.
  • The speaker and mic are at the bottom edge of the phone: play over that end, not over the top.
  • Hold a hand still at 10 cm: a flat bright line at 10 cm. Move it up and down: the line climbs and falls.
  • A smudge right at the bottom is normal: that's the phone hearing its own speaker.

How to play

  1. Phone flat, screen up, volume set first (changing it re-measures the table). Tap Start sonar, nothing above it for 1 s.
  2. Click Enable sound here.
  3. Hold a book flat over the phone's bottom edge. Hold it where the lowest note should be and click Set low note here; lift it to where the highest should be (up to about 35 cm) and click Set high note here.
  4. Easy snaps to 8 notes that always sound good together; Theremin glides. Take the book away to go quiet.
  5. Tap Stop on the phone when done: the session is saved, so tell me how it felt and I'll replay it.
